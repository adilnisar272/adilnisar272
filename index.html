Common questions

Working with a senior full-stack developer, wherever you are

Which stacks do you work on?
Yes. After 8+ years I join the stack the team already has: MERN, MEAN, MEVN, PERN, LAMP, Laravel, Next.js, Nuxt, NestJS, Django, FastAPI, Spring Boot, .NET, and WordPress. Frontend, backend, mobile, data and cloud are all in range.
Can I hire you as a Laravel, MERN, React, or AWS developer?
Yes. Those are the searches this work already covers: Laravel, PHP, MERN, React, Next.js, Node.js, Angular, Vue, WordPress, Django, and AWS, plus AI integrations, REST APIs, e-commerce, and payment gateways such as Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, PayFast and Paysafe.
Do you handle AI integrations and cloud infrastructure?
Yes. I integrate AI into products, train and fine-tune LLMs, and build retrieval and prompt workflows. On the infrastructure side I work with AWS, Azure and GCP: compute, storage, networking, Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD.
How do you lead a team?
I build the team, then I bond it, then I lead the work. I stay in the code, share the credit, and make the goal obvious. If you want to go far, you go together.
Who will I talk to, and who writes the code?
On a direct project you talk to me, and I write the code. If the engagement is a lead role, I still stay in the code, and you still have one person accountable for the work.
Can you join a product that already exists?
Yes. Most of the work is on products that already have a stack, a database and a team. I read what is there, keep the parts that are sound, and change what the product actually needs.
How do you price work, and how long does it take?
I quote after I understand the product, the stack, and whether you need a builder, a lead, or both. A defined product is priced by milestones. Joining your team is a full-time arrangement. I do not give a duration before I have seen the scope, and if a date starts to slip I say so while there is still a choice.
What if we need to change the scope?
A fix to something we already agreed is part of the work. A new feature is new scope. I describe it and price it before I build it, so it does not show up as a surprise at the end.
Who owns the code?
You do. I prefer the repository to live in your GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket from the start, with me invited in. At handover you keep the code, the history, and the accounts the product runs on, plus a short note on how to run it.
How do we communicate across time zones?
You get me directly, in English, not an account manager. I am based in Lahore, GMT+5, which overlaps the Gulf, Europe and much of Asia, and I set an evening window for a US morning. I also work on site in your city, or relocate. Updates are written so you can read them on your hours, and we call when a decision needs a call.
Can you build a store and take payments?
Yes. I have built stores such as C-Basket, and I work with Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, PayFast, Paysafe and similar gateways. Card data stays with the payment provider.
Will you sign an NDA, and what do you need to start?
Yes. I sign an NDA before private product details are shared. To start I need one person who can make decisions, access to the existing systems, and a plain description of what the first version must do.
Where are you based, and can you work in my country?
I am based in Lahore, Pakistan, on GMT+5, and I am available in every region. That means remote work for a team anywhere, on-site work with that team, or a relocation for the role. I take full-time roles, defined projects, and freelance work. On Upwork I am a top-ranked freelancer. Send a request through the form and I will follow up.

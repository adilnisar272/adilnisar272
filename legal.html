Disclaimer

Client, company and product work

Where a company, product, or public website is named, it is there so a reader can see the kind of work he has done with employers and clients. Ownership of those products, brands, codebases, and content stays with the client, the employer, or the other owner under their contracts.

Naming a company is a description of work. It is not a statement that the company employs him today, sponsors him, or endorses this portfolio. Employment dates on the site are the dates he has recorded for each role.

  • Screenshots of public marketing sites show those sites so the product is recognisable. A page can change after the screenshot was taken.

Privacy

This portfolio does not run an account system, and it does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The light and dark theme stays in the browser for the visit and is not stored in an account.

If you use the contact form, the site sends the name, email address, the kind of request you choose, and the message you write. Delivery goes through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which passes the message on so he can reply to the address you entered. He uses that message to respond to the enquiry. He does not sell it.

A honeypot field is included to catch automated spam and is not meant to be filled in. The downloadable CV is a file you choose to open. It can contain the contact details he placed in the résumé.

To ask about a message you sent, use the contact form on the home page and say what you want changed or deleted.

Claims, corrections and takedown requests

If you represent a client, a company, or another rights holder and you want a name, a screenshot, or a sentence changed or removed, send the request through the contact form. Include the page address, the item you mean, and whether you want a correction or a removal.

He will read the request and update the portfolio when the request is fair. A request is the right first step. This page is here so the limit of each claim on the portfolio is visible before anyone has to guess.

If a matter needs formal legal handling, have a qualified lawyer review it. This page states how the portfolio should be read. It does not replace advice on the law of a particular country.
